How the error is measured

Align the shaft, then compare the condyles

  1. 1

    Identify

    The post-op scan is matched against all plannings for that specimen. The condyles pick the right one unambiguously.

  2. 2

    Align the shaft

    The scanned proximal shaft is registered onto the planned shaft. The shaft becomes the common reference.

  3. 3

    Fit the distal head

    The distal fragment is fitted on its own. The rigid motion from the planned head to the achieved head is the error.

  4. 4

    Report

    The residual rotation is split about the condylar, AP and shaft axes. Translation is reported at the knee centre and at the osteotomy.

Sign convention: a positive component is in the same direction as the planned correction, so + means over-corrected and − means under-corrected.
